### Born from Personal Experience

![French Riviera Tips Home Page Hero view](/images/body/french-riviera-on-a-budget-1-c.jpg)
*French Riviera Tips - Your Travel Blog born from personal experience*

**Why French Riviera Tips?** I created French Riviera Tips after living in Nice since 2020 and witnessing a recurring pattern: every year, more than 5 million people visit the French Riviera, drawn by its legendary beauty and glamorous reputation. The region is famous (or perhaps infamous) for being expensive, with destinations like [Monaco](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Tourism-g190405-Monaco-Vacations.html), [Saint-Tropez](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Tourism-g187242-Saint_Tropez_French_Riviera_Cote_d_Azur_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur-Vacations.html), and [Èze](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Tourism-g187222-Eze_French_Riviera_Cote_d_Azur_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur-Vacations.html) commanding premium prices that can quickly drain travel budgets.

But here's what I've discovered during my years living here: the Côte d'Azur has two faces:

There's the glossy, expensive side that everyone knows about, and then there's the authentic Riviera (equally beautiful), often more memorable, and significantly more affordable.

By exploring every corner of this region, I've spotted countless places that deliver exceptional quality and satisfaction without the inflated price tags.

[French Riviera Tips](https://www.frenchrivieratips.com) exists to share these discoveries with you. Yes, we feature the well-known luxury hotels, famous restaurants, and classic activities—because some experiences truly are worth the splurge.

But our real passion lies in revealing the hidden gems: the family-run restaurant where locals eat, the secluded beach without the €30 sunbed rental, the charming guesthouse with views rivaling five-star hotels at a fraction of the cost, the authentic experiences that create unforgettable memories without breaking the bank.

My mission is simple: help you discover one of the most famous regions in the world while spending less, feeling satisfied, and experiencing the authentic soul of the French Riviera that most tourists never find.

Enjoy your stay!
