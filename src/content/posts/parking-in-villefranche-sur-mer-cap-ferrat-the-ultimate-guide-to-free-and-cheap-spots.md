*Villefranche-Sur-Mer — Where to park?*

Finding a parking spot in [**Villefranche-sur-Mer**](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Tourism-g187246-Villefranche_sur_Mer_French_Riviera_Cote_d_Azur_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur-Vacations.html) or [**Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat**](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Tourism-g187237-St_Jean_Cap_Ferrat_French_Riviera_Cote_d_Azur_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur-Vacations.html) can feel like a mission impossible, especially during the sun-drenched months of the high season. With narrow medieval streets and a high demand for coastal access, knowing exactly where to go before you start your engine is the best way to save time and avoid heavy fines.

### At a Glance: Parking Essentials

If you're in a rush, here is the quick breakdown of the parking situation:

- **Best for Beach Access:** Parking de la Marinière (Villefranche).
- **Average Cost:** €1.60 to €2.50 per hour in central zones.
- **Best Free Option:** Upper residential roads (Corne d'Or) or walking from [Beaulieu-sur-Mer](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Tourism-g187218-Beaulieu_sur_Mer_French_Riviera_Cote_d_Azur_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur-Vacations.html).
- **Must-Have App:** Download [**Flowbird**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=group.flowbird.mpp&pli=1) or [**PayByPhone**](https://www.paybyphone.com) to top up your parking remotely.

In this guide, we draw on our local experience to show you exactly where the "secret" spots are, how to decode French parking signs, and how to enjoy the Côte d'Azur without spending a fortune on parking meters. Whether you're here for a quick swim at Marinière beach or a glamorous lunch at the Saint-Jean harbor, we've got you covered.

## Is there Free Parking in Villefranche-sur-Mer?

The short answer is: **yes, but you have to earn it.** While the waterfront is almost exclusively paid parking, there are a few local secrets to keep your wallet happy.

## The "Corne d'Or" Area

The golden rule of parking in Villefranche is simple: **The higher you go, the cheaper it gets.** As you drive up away from the harbor toward the **Corne d'Or** residential area (along the Moyenne Corniche), you will find street parking with white lines. Many of these spots are free and offer breathtaking views of the bay—though you should be prepared for a steep 15-minute walk back up from the beach!

## The Secret "Cemetery" Spot

If you want to avoid the chaos of the port, head toward the [**Cimetière de Villefranche**](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5Tt6tzKYumNbu4WS6) (the local cemetery). There is often more availability in the surrounding streets. It is a much quieter alternative, and the walk down to the Old Town is surprisingly scenic.

## Off-Season Rules

If you are visiting between **November and March**, parking regulations are much more relaxed. While most central spots remain paid during the day, always check the local meters. On certain **public holidays** or Sundays in the deep off-season, you may find that some zones do not require payment—but always read the "Horodateur" (parking meter) screen to be sure.

## Best Paid Car Parks in Villefranche (Closest to the Beach)

Sometimes, time is more valuable than money. If you have kids, beach gear, or simply want to be in the water in under two minutes, these are your best bets:

- [**Parking de la Marinière**](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g187246-d7054730-Reviews-Plage_de_la_Mariniere-Villefranche_sur_Mer_French_Riviera_Cote_d_Azur_Provence_Al.html): This is the "holy grail" of beach parking. It is located directly at the foot of the main beach (Plage des Marinières).
  - **Rate:** Expect to pay approximately **€2.20 per hour**.
  - **Warning:** It fills up by 10:00 AM in the summer!
- [**Parking Wilson**](https://maps.app.goo.gl/x1TEpkE1bgwzPYcL7): Located right next to the Citadel and the entrance to the Old Town. This is the most convenient spot for a romantic dinner or a visit to the Saint-Pierre Chapel.

> **Pro Tip:** Don't waste time fumbling for coins. Download the [**Flowbird**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=group.flowbird.mpp&pli=1) or [**PayByPhone**](https://www.paybyphone.com) app. You can start, stop, and extend your parking session directly from your towel on the beach.

## Parking in Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat: A Local's Secret

Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat is even more exclusive and narrower than Villefranche. Finding a spot here requires a strategy.

## Place du Centenaire

This is the main parking lot at the port. It's incredibly convenient for the village restaurants, but it is often full by mid-morning. If you see a spot here, take it immediately—don't "loop around" hoping for better, as you likely won't find one.

## Passable Beach Parking

For those visiting the famous [**Plage de Passable**](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g187237-d1872606-Reviews-Plage_de_Passable-St_Jean_Cap_Ferrat_French_Riviera_Cote_d_Azur_Provence_Alpes_Co.html?m=69573), there is a small dirt lot nearby. It is often cheaper than the port parking, and if you arrive very early in the morning (before 8:30 AM), you might even find a few free roadside spots before the official paid zone begins.

## The "Walking Strategy" (Highly Recommended)

If Cap Ferrat is totally congested, park in the neighboring town of [**Beaulieu-sur-Mer**](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Tourism-g187218-Beaulieu_sur_Mer_French_Riviera_Cote_d_Azur_Provence_Alpes_Cote_d_Azur-Vacations.html) (near the train station or the port). From there, take the [**Promenade Maurice Rouvier**](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g187218-d4674831-Reviews-Promenade_Maurice_Rouvier-Beaulieu_sur_Mer_French_Riviera_Cote_d_Azur_Provence_Al.html). It is a flat, paved, and incredibly scenic coastal path that leads you straight into the heart of Saint-Jean in just 15 minutes.

## Practical Information & Tips

To avoid a "fine" souvenir from France, keep these rules in mind:

- **White Lines:** General parking. Look for the word *"Payant"* on the ground. If it's there, you must pay.
- **Blue Zones:** Requires a parking disc (available in newsagents) for limited-time free parking.
- **Yellow Lines:** Strictly prohibited (reserved for deliveries, buses, or emergency vehicles).
- **Peak Hours:** During July and August, aim to arrive **before 9:30 AM**. After this, you will likely spend 30 minutes just circling.
- **Fines:** In France, a parking fine is called a *Forfait Post-Stationnement (FPS)*. In this region, they typically cost between **€25 and €35**.

## FAQ: Parking on the French Riviera

**Is parking free on Sundays in Villefranche-sur-Mer?** Generally, no. In the high season, parking remains paid every day of the week in the city center and near the beaches to ensure a turnover of vehicles.

**Can I park a motorhome (RV) in Cap Ferrat?** It is extremely difficult. The roads are very narrow with tight turns. We strongly recommend parking your RV at a designated campsite in Antibes or Villeneuve-Loubet and taking the coastal train (TER) to Villefranche or Beaulieu.
