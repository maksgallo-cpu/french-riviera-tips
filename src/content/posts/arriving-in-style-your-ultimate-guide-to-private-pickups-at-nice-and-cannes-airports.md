## Nice Côte d'Azur Airport (NCE): Navigating Terminal 1 and Terminal 2

As the third busiest airport in France, Nice Airport can be a maze. Most travelers arrive via Terminal 1 (international carriers) or Terminal 2 (Air France, EasyJet, and others).

### Where to Meet Your Driver at Terminal 1

If your flight lands at Terminal 1, you will exit through the sliding doors after collecting your luggage. For pre-booked **Welcome Pickups** or private "Meet & Greet" services, your driver will be standing just behind the arrival barrier holding a sign with your name.

- **The Pickup Zone:** If your driver has instructed you to meet at a specific "Pickup" point, you need to head toward **Gate A0**.
- **Pro Tip:** Terminal 1 is slightly more compact than Terminal 2. However, during the Cannes Film Festival, the arrivals hall can get incredibly crowded. Always keep your phone on.

### Where to Meet Your Driver at Terminal 2

Terminal 2 is larger and serves many major European airlines. After exiting the baggage claim, you will find yourself in a wide arrivals hall.

- **The Meeting Point:** For traditional private transfers, drivers wait in front of **Exit A3**.
- **Rideshare & App Pickups:** If you have booked via an app, you will likely be directed to the **"Parking Pro" or "P5" area**. Follow signs for "Taxis/Chauffeurs."
- **The Tram Link:** There is a free shuttle tram that connects T1 and T2 every few minutes if you find yourself at the wrong terminal.

## Cannes-Mandelieu Airport (CEQ): The Gateway for Private Aviation

Unlike the commercial volume in Nice, **Cannes-Mandelieu Airport** is the choice for private charters. It is located just 6km from the center of Cannes.

### The Pickup Experience at Cannes-Mandelieu

The pickup here is a model of discretion and efficiency.

- **Direct Tarmac Access:** In many cases, VIP transfers are authorized to meet you directly at the steps of the aircraft or the FBO lounge.
- **The Main Terminal:** For standard private transfers, the meeting point is directly outside the main terminal entrance.
