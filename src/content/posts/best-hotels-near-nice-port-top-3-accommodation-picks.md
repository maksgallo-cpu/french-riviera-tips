After several years exploring the hotels of Nice, I've tested many establishments in different neighborhoods. Today, I'm sharing my three favorite addresses near the Nice port. These are hotels I've personally experienced, and they truly deserve a place in your travel notebook!

<!-- STAY22 MAP EMBED: https://www.stay22.com/embed/6952e17d30df42e10d3b74f3 -->

## 1. [Hôtel du Pin](https://expedia.stay22.com/frt25/-TcMrRzxfe): the ideal choice to live Nice at its heart!

![Hotel Du Pin Nice bedroom](https://expedia.stay22.com/frt25/-TcMrRzxfe)
*Hotel Du Port Nice - Picture courtesy of Booking.com*

[Hôtel du Pin](https://expedia.stay22.com/frt25/-TcMrRzxfe) is a 3-star boutique hotel from the Soniho group, ideally located on Place du Pin in the dynamic port neighborhood. It's an establishment renovated in 2020 that combines modern comfort with conviviality.

What makes this hotel exceptional is its perfect location. You're in the immediate proximity of the Promenade des Anglais, the Old Town of Nice, and the marina. Access to the airport is also very easy thanks to tram line 2. For business travelers, the hotel offers Scool, a dedicated professional space with 7 meeting rooms and a free coworking space for guests.

[Hôtel du Pin](https://expedia.stay22.com/frt25/-TcMrRzxfe) also features FOAM restaurant, an original craft beer bar with 12 self-serve beer taps where you can enjoy all-day dining. It's the perfect place to unwind after a day of sightseeing.

**About the Soniho Group**: The group manages four hotels on the French Riviera: [Hôtel du Pin](https://expedia.stay22.com/frt25/-TcMrRzxfe) in Nice, Hôtel Isidore (Nice West), Hôtel Abrial (Cannes), and Hôtel des Victoires (Cannes-la-Bocca). It's a beautiful family chain deeply rooted in the territory, prioritizing authenticity and personalized hospitality.

## 2. [Palais Séguranne](https://expedia.stay22.com/frt25/-_NqVdXNBV): Luxury and Intimacy Near Castle Hill

![Bedroom of Palais Segurane Hotel Nice](https://expedia.stay22.com/frt25/-_NqVdXNBV)
*Palais Segurane Nice - Picture courtesy of Booking.com*

[Palais Séguranne](https://expedia.stay22.com/frt25/-_NqVdXNBV) is a 4-star boutique hotel in the heart of Old Nice, at the foot of the majestic Castle Hill. With its 43 spacious suites, this establishment offers a refined setting combining contemporary design and French elegance.

What makes the charm of [Palais Séguranne](https://expedia.stay22.com/frt25/-_NqVdXNBV) is its intimate and sophisticated atmosphere. The suites are truly sumptuous and fully equipped with modern kitchens. The hotel offers high-end services: a private spa with sauna and hammam, a starred restaurant (Catherine), and a private cinema room where you can watch your favorite films.

The location is incomparable: you're just minutes away from Cours Saleya, the Bay of Angels, and close to all the treasures of Old Nice. It's the ideal place for a romantic getaway or an unforgettable wellness retreat.

**Discover Its Sister Hotel**: [Palais Séguranne](https://expedia.stay22.com/frt25/-_NqVdXNBV) has a sister establishment, [Palais Saleya](https://expedia.stay22.com/frt25/MJuHey-jkY), located at Palace of Justice Square, at the entrance to Old Nice. This 4-star boutique hotel features a magnificently restored 18th-century building with 26 suites and apartments. It's another excellent option if you're looking for this style of luxury accommodation in the historic heart of Nice.

## 3. [Hôtel Esprit d'Azur](https://expedia.stay22.com/frt25/F0WUImRjWe): Authenticity and Proximity to the City Center

![bedroom Hotel Esprit d'Azur Nice](https://expedia.stay22.com/frt25/F0WUImRjWe)
*Hotel Esprit d'Azur Nice - Picture courtesy of Expedia*

[Hôtel Esprit d'Azur](https://expedia.stay22.com/frt25/F0WUImRjWe) is an independent and authentic hotel with 37 rooms, located right in the heart of Nice's city center. It's a family-run establishment led with passion by a team that prioritizes warm hospitality and quality service.

This hotel stands out for its convenient location: it's only a 5-minute walk from Old Nice and Garibaldi Square, and very close to the République tram stop (line L1), which allows you to easily access all points of interest in Nice. The Promenade des Anglais is accessible by tram in just a few minutes, as are the beaches.

The rooms are comfortable and well-equipped with free Wi-Fi, flat-screen TV, and most importantly, the hotel offers an excellent breakfast buffet. The reception and bar are open 24/7, and the staff is renowned for being always available and helpful. The hotel also offers on-site parking (paid), ideal if you're traveling by car.

## My Overall Opinion

These three hotels offer very different but all excellent experiences, depending on what you're looking for. If you want a modern, well-located boutique hotel with business services, Hôtel du Pin is your answer. If you dream of a luxurious and romantic stay with spa facilities, choose [Palais Séguranne](https://expedia.stay22.com/frt25/-_NqVdXNBV) or its sister hotel [Palais Saleya](https://expedia.stay22.com/frt25/MJuHey-jkY). And if you're seeking authenticity, city center proximity, and excellent value for money, [Hôtel Esprit d'Azur](https://expedia.stay22.com/frt25/F0WUImRjWe) is the ideal choice.

All three hotels offer easy access to the port, Old Nice, and the Promenade des Anglais. You can't go wrong choosing one of these three establishments for your stay on the French Riviera!

Enjoy your stay in Nice! 🏨
